Eating out in Wavecrest and J-Bay

Where to eat in Jeffreys Bay

Greystone is in Wavecrest, behind the Spar, with a supermarket and restaurants within 100 m. Here's where to eat on foot, and a few places worth the short drive.

No car needed

Within walking distance of Greystone

Nina's is right across the road from our front door, so nobody needs to drive after dinner.

across the road

Nina's Real Food

In the Wavecrest Centre at 126 Da Gama Road, right across the road from Greystone. Rated 4.4 out of 5 by more than 1,400 reviewers on TripAdvisor (October 2026).

A short drive

Further afield

Jeffreys Bay Tourism lists these among the town's restaurants. Opening times change with the season, so check with each restaurant before you go.

Kitchen Windows Beach Restaurant

On the beach next to the Kitchen Windows surf spot on Dolphin Beach, where the beginners' surf lessons happen.

Tasty Table Cafe

A family-run cafe overlooking Dolphin Beach, the town's main beach.

Seashells Strandloper

A coffee shop and restaurant on Da Gama Road, close to Wavecrest.

De Viswijf

A Jeffreys Bay restaurant established in 1999.

Breakfast at Greystone

Our rates are room-only, and a full cooked South African breakfast is available to order each morning: eggs, bacon, sausages, toast with preserves, and fresh coffee and tea. Have it in the dining room or out on the balcony, then walk down to the beach.

Groceries

The Spar supermarket is right in front of Greystone, for picnic supplies, snacks and anything you forgot to pack. The outside rooms (7–8) have a microwave, fridge and kettle, and every room has a tea and coffee maker. Grocery deliveries can be arranged at an extra charge.
