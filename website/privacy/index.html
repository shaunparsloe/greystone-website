Privacy notice

This website has no forms and no logins, and it never asks you to type in personal details. It does use two analytics services to see how visitors use the site. This page explains what they collect and how to switch them off.

Who we are

Greystone Guesthouse, 11 Mimosa Street, Wavecrest, Jeffreys Bay, 6330, South Africa, is responsible for this website. For anything on this page, WhatsApp or call us on +27 82 953 7641.

What the website collects

On every page we use:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google). It counts which pages are visited, how visitors arrived (for example from a Google search), their device, browser and approximate location (country and city), and clicks on the Book Now, WhatsApp and phone links. It sets cookies named _ga and _ga_… to tell repeat visits apart.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft). It records how pages are used (clicks, taps, scrolling and mouse movement), as session recordings and heatmaps, so we can see where the site is hard to use. It sets cookies named _clck and _clsk.

We use this only to understand and improve the website. We see reports and recordings of visits, not your name or contact details. Both scripts load after the page has finished loading.

Google and Microsoft process this data for us under their own terms: see Google's privacy policy, how Google uses data from sites that use its services, and the Microsoft privacy statement.

Our fonts and photos come from this website itself, so no font or image service sees your visit.

How to switch analytics off

  • Open any page with ?notrack=1 at the end of the address. Google Analytics and Clarity then stay off on that browser and device. Use ?notrack=0 to turn them back on. The setting is saved in your browser, so clearing your browser's site data resets it.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a content blocker. The website works the same without them.

Bookings and messages

Bookings made with the Book Now button are handled by NightsBridge, our booking system, and bookings on Booking.com by Booking.com. If you message us on WhatsApp or call us, WhatsApp and your phone provider handle that conversation. Each has its own privacy policy. The details you give when you book or get in touch are used to arrange your stay and answer your questions.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. To do that, WhatsApp or call us on +27 82 953 7641. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Regulator.

If this notice changes, we'll update the date at the top of the page.
