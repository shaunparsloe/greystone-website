For contractors and corporate guests

Business stays in Jeffreys Bay

Working in Jeffreys Bay, Humansdorp or the wider Kouga area? Greystone gives you a quiet en-suite room, WiFi that stays on through load shedding, free parking at the door and the beach at the end of the day.

Built for a working stay

  • Power that stays on. Solar power with an inverter and battery backup keeps the lights and WiFi running during load shedding, so calls and deadlines aren't at the mercy of Eskom.
  • Free WiFi in every room.
  • Free private parking on site, with no need to reserve, and street parking as well.
  • Quiet hours from 21:00 to 09:00, for an early night before an early start.
  • En-suite rooms with a flat-screen TV and tea and coffee maker, and daily housekeeping.
  • Check-in from 14:00 to 20:00; please let us know your arrival time. Check-out is between 07:00 and 10:00.
  • Breakfast is available to order each morning; rates are room-only.

Single-occupancy rates

Travelling alone? You pay a single rate: from R650 a night in the queen rooms (5–6) and the outside rooms (7–8), and from R700 in the deluxe sea-view rooms (1–4). The outside rooms have their own entrance and a small kitchenette with a microwave, fridge and kettle, which suits longer stays.

Rates change with the season. See all rooms and rates, or check live availability for your dates.

Payment and paperwork

Payment is by bank transfer before arrival. We accept Visa, Mastercard and Bankcard, but not cash. If your company needs an invoice or other paperwork, please ask Monique when you book.

Longer stays and teams

Different policies apply when you book more than 2 rooms. If you're bringing a team or staying for a few weeks, WhatsApp or call us on +27 82 953 7641 before you book.

Getting here and getting around

  • Airport: Chief Dawid Stuurman International Airport in Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha) is 64 km away, about an hour's drive along the N2.
  • Humansdorp is about 15 minutes' drive from Greystone, and the rest of the Kouga area is within easy reach.
  • In town: Greystone is in Wavecrest, behind the Spar, with a supermarket and restaurants within 100 m. See our restaurant guide for dinner within walking distance.

Staying at Greystone? Monique will help you arrange an airport shuttle from Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha) and can put you in touch with local operators.

Questions from business guests

Does the WiFi work during load shedding?

Yes. Greystone runs on solar power with an inverter and battery backup, so the lights and WiFi stay on through Eskom power cuts, and you can keep working.

Do you have single-occupancy rates?

Yes. A room for one guest starts from R650 a night in the queen and outside rooms, and from R700 in a deluxe room. Rates change with the season; check live availability for your dates.

How far is Greystone from Humansdorp?

Humansdorp is about 15 minutes' drive from Greystone. If you're working in Humansdorp or elsewhere in the Kouga area, you can stay by the sea in Jeffreys Bay and drive in each day.

Can I get an invoice for my stay?

Please ask Monique when you book, so she can make sure you get the paperwork your company needs.

Can you take bookings for a team?

Yes. Different policies apply when you book more than 2 rooms, so WhatsApp or call us first and we'll talk you through it.
