Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How far is Greystone Guesthouse from Supertubes beach?

Greystone Guesthouse is just 260 metres (about a 3-minute walk) from the beach and 300 metres from Supertubes, one of the world's most famous surf breaks. We're located in the Wavecrest area of Jeffreys Bay, with restaurants and shops within 100 metres.

What accommodation options are available?

We offer 8 en-suite rooms across three tiers: Deluxe Sea View rooms (Rooms 1–4) with balconies and sea views from ZAR 1,200/night, Standard Queen rooms (Rooms 5–6) from ZAR 1,000/night, and Budget Annex rooms (Rooms 7–8) with private entrances and kitchenettes from ZAR 1,000/night. Single occupancy rates start from ZAR 650/night.

Is Greystone a hotel or a guest house?

Greystone is a 3-star graded B&B guest house rather than a hotel. You get what you'd expect from a Jeffreys Bay hotel (en-suite rooms, daily housekeeping, free WiFi and secure parking) with a host on site who looks after every guest personally.

Do you have affordable accommodation in Jeffreys Bay?

Yes. Our Standard Queen and Budget Annex rooms start at ZAR 1,000 per night for two people, and single occupancy starts at ZAR 650 per night. Every room is en-suite with free WiFi and parking, and power stays on during load shedding.

Where should surfers stay in Jeffreys Bay?

Wavecrest, the area right behind Supertubes and The Point, puts you closest to the best waves. Greystone is 300 metres from Supertubes, so you can check the surf from the balcony and be in the water in minutes.

How far is Greystone from Humansdorp?

Humansdorp is about 15 minutes' drive from Greystone. If you're working in Humansdorp or the Kouga area, you can stay by the sea in Jeffreys Bay, with free parking, fast WiFi and solar backup.

Is breakfast included at Greystone Guesthouse?

Rates are room-only, with a full cooked South African breakfast available to order each morning — eggs, bacon, sausages, toast, fresh coffee and tea. Enjoy it in the dining room or on the balcony with sea views.

Do you have free parking and WiFi?

Yes — Greystone Guesthouse offers free private on-site parking (no reservation needed) and free WiFi throughout the property, including in all guest rooms.

What happens during load shedding?

Nothing changes for our guests. Greystone Guesthouse has a full solar power system with inverter and battery backup, so your lights, WiFi, and hot water stay on through Eskom power outages. You can continue working, streaming and using your devices without interruption.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00 to 20:00 (please let us know your arrival time in advance). Check-out is between 07:00 and 10:00.

What activities are available near Jeffreys Bay?

Jeffreys Bay offers world-class surfing at Supertubes (300m away), beach horse riding, skydiving over the Indian Ocean, ADDO Elephant Park day trips, lion sanctuary visits, snorkelling, diving, fishing, windsurfing, golf, and hiking. Restaurants and a supermarket are within 100 metres of the guesthouse.

How do I get to Greystone from the airport?

We're 64 km (about an hour's drive) from Chief Dawid Stuurman International Airport in Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha). Monique will help you arrange an airport shuttle and can put you in touch with local operators: just send her a WhatsApp.
