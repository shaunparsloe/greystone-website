Five minutes on foot

The walk to Supertubes

From Greystone it's about 300 m to Supertubes and 260 m to the beach. There's no need to load the car or find parking at the break: grab your board, walk down the street and you're there. Point Beach, beside the Point break, is about 450 m away.

Being this close changes a surf trip. You can check the waves first thing, go back for coffee, and paddle out when the tide and wind line up. When the session's done, the shower, your room and lunch are a five-minute walk away.

When to come

Supertubes is a long, fast right-hand point break, and it needs a solid groundswell to come alive. Those swells arrive mostly in the South African winter, roughly May to September, when cold fronts send long-period swell up the coast. That's the season for the long, hollow walls J-Bay is famous for.

Summer (roughly October to April) has smaller, more mixed surf, warmer water and long evenings, and it can still have good days. Whatever the season, mornings are usually the calmest time to be in the water.

The water is cool for most of the year, so most surfers wear a full wetsuit, especially in winter.

The J-Bay Open

The World Surf League's J-Bay Open, part of the Championship Tour, is usually held at Supertubes in July, alongside the J-Bay Surf Festival. Spectators watch from the shore, right beside the wave.

Dates change every year, so check the WSL website before you plan. Jeffreys Bay fills up during the contest, so book early if you'd like to walk to it.

Learning to surf

Supertubes is for experienced surfers. If you're learning, head to Kitchen Windows on Dolphin Beach, about 1.1 km from Greystone, where the waves are gentler and the surf schools teach.

  • Wavecrest Surf School teaches at Kitchen Windows.
  • Learn 2 Surf also teaches at Kitchen Windows.

Lesson times and prices change with the season and the tides, so contact the schools for current details. If you already surf but travelled light, several shops in town hire out boards and wetsuits.

Staying at Greystone? Monique will help you arrange surf lessons or board and wetsuit hire, and can put you in touch with the right people for the season.

Getting here with your boards

Fly into Chief Dawid Stuurman International Airport in Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha), 64 km from Greystone, about an hour's drive along the N2. A hire car makes it easy to chase the swell to other breaks, and we have free private parking on site. If you'd rather not drive, an airport shuttle is the simplest way to get you and your boards here.

Surf trip questions

How far is Greystone from Supertubes?

About 300 m, a five-minute walk. The beach itself is 260 m from our front door, so you can check the waves and be paddling out a few minutes later.

When is the best time to surf Jeffreys Bay?

The biggest and most consistent swells come in the South African winter, roughly May to September. Summer has smaller, more mixed surf, but warmer water and long evenings.

Can beginners surf Supertubes?

No: Supertubes is a fast, powerful wave for experienced surfers. Beginners learn at Kitchen Windows on Dolphin Beach, about 1.1 km from Greystone, where surf schools teach on gentler waves.

When is the J-Bay Open?

The World Surf League's J-Bay Open is usually held at Supertubes in July. Dates change each year, so check the WSL website, and book early: the town fills up during the contest.

Can I store my surfboard at Greystone?

Please ask Monique about board storage and rinsing when you book.

Can I have breakfast before an early surf?

Breakfast is available to order each morning. If you're planning a dawn session, ask Monique what time breakfast can be ready.
