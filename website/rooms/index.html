Rooms & rates at Greystone, Jeffreys Bay

Eight en-suite rooms 260 m from the beach and 300 m from Supertubes: four deluxe rooms with balconies and sea views, two queen rooms and two outside rooms with their own entrance. Live rates and availability are one tap away.

Rooms 1–4 · Sea view

Deluxe Sea View rooms

From R1,200 a night for 2 guests · from R700 for 1

Our four deluxe rooms have a balcony or patio and a partial view of the sea over Wavecrest. Mornings start with coffee outside and a look at the surf.

  • Beds: deluxe king (extra-large double), deluxe double (large double), deluxe room with patio, and room 3, the family room, with a double and a single bed
  • Size: about 24 m² (258 ft²)
  • In the room: en-suite bathroom, flat-screen TV, free WiFi, tea and coffee maker
  • Best for: couples, and anyone who wants the sea view
Check availability & book

Rooms 5–6 · No view

Queen rooms

From R1,000 a night for 2 guests · from R650 for 1

Comfortable, quiet en-suite rooms in the main house for guests who'd rather spend the money on the sea than on a view of it. Supertubes is still a five-minute walk away.

  • Beds: room 5 has a queen bed; room 6 has twin beds
  • In the room: en-suite bathroom, flat-screen TV, free WiFi, tea and coffee maker
  • Best for: surfers, friends sharing (room 6) and business stays
Check availability & book

Rooms 7–8 · Ground floor annex

Outside rooms with their own entrance

From R1,000 a night for 2 guests · from R650 for 1

Two ground-floor rooms in the annex, each with a private entrance and a small kitchenette, so you can come and go for the dawn session or keep your own hours.

  • Beds: room 7 is a twin room and room 8 a double room
  • Size: about 15 m² (161 ft²)
  • In the room: en-suite bathroom, flat-screen TV, free WiFi, microwave, fridge, electric kettle and coffee machine
  • Best for: longer stays, independent travellers and anyone who'd rather avoid stairs

What every room has

  • En-suite bathroom, with free toiletries, towels and linen
  • Flat-screen TV and free WiFi
  • Tea and coffee maker
  • Daily housekeeping
  • Free private parking on site, with no need to reserve
  • Solar power with battery backup, so your lights and WiFi stay on during load shedding

Around the house you'll find a shared lounge with TV, a games room with a billiards table and darts, board games and puzzles, an outdoor fireplace and braai area, and a safety deposit box. There's no fence or locked gate: it's a relaxed, open house.

Breakfast

Rates are room-only. A full cooked South African breakfast is available to order each morning: eggs, bacon, sausages, toast with preserves, and fresh coffee and tea. Have it in the dining room or out on the balcony.

House rules

  • Check-in: 14:00–20:00. Please let us know your arrival time in advance.
  • Check-out: 07:00–10:00
  • Quiet hours: 21:00–09:00
  • Age: guests under 18 must be with a parent or guardian
  • No pets, no smoking and no parties
  • Payment: by bank transfer before arrival. We accept Visa, Mastercard and Bankcard, but not cash.
  • Groups: different policies apply when you book more than 2 rooms

Questions about the rooms

Is Greystone a hotel or a guest house?

Greystone is a 3-star graded B&B guest house, not a hotel. You get what you'd look for in a Jeffreys Bay hotel (en-suite rooms, daily housekeeping, free WiFi and secure parking) with a host on site who looks after every guest personally.

Do you have affordable rooms in Jeffreys Bay?

Yes. The queen rooms (5–6) and the outside rooms (7–8) start from R1,000 a night for two people, and from R650 for one. Every room is en-suite, with free WiFi and parking, and the power stays on during load shedding.

Which rooms have a sea view?

The four deluxe rooms (1–4) have a balcony or patio and a partial sea view. The queen rooms and the outside rooms have no sea view.

Do you have single rates?

Yes. One guest pays from R700 a night in a deluxe room and from R650 in the other rooms. Check live availability for your dates, as rates change with the season.

Do you have twin rooms?

Room 6 has twin beds, and outside room 7 is listed as a twin room. Book them by room name on our booking page, or WhatsApp us if you're not sure which room suits you.

Are there ground-floor rooms with no stairs?

Yes. The two outside rooms (7–8) are on the ground floor, with their own entrance, so there are no stairs to climb.
